# Trademark Clearance — SEARCH PREP for "DecentralFlix"
**Decentralflix Lifeboat — Legal Basics** | Prepared 2026-09-28 | **NOT LEGAL ADVICE. Needs a lawyer's read before launch.**

> This document is **search preparation only**. Nothing has been filed. No trademark application, registration, or legal conclusion is expressed here. A trademark attorney must run and interpret the actual search.

## The strategy doc's flag

From the plan of record ("DecentralFlix: How to Corner a Market in 2026"):

- *"'Decentral' describes plumbing, and '-Flix' invites a Netflix comparison."*
- *"'Flix' marks do register for streaming, as FLIXOLÉ shows, so it isn't blocked outright."* (source cited in the strategy doc: Justia — FLIXOLÉ trademark)

The 90-day plan lists "a trademark clearance search on the name" as required legal basics. This doc preps that search; it does not substitute for it.

## Search strategy (for counsel to execute/confirm)

### 1. USPTO TESS (federal register)
- Exact search: **"DecentralFlix"** (and common variants: "Decentral Flix", "DECENTRALFLIX", "DecentralFlicks").
- Confusingly-similar search: marks containing "Decentral" in entertainment/streaming, and "Flix"-formative marks in the relevant classes. Pay specific attention to marks owned by large streamers.
- Search both live and dead marks — dead marks still inform risk, and abandoned filings can be revived or indicate crowded space.

### 2. Relevant classes — starting points for counsel to confirm, NOT definitive
Suggest to counsel as a starting point (counsel decides the actual filing/search classes):
- **Class 041** — entertainment services (film exhibition, distribution)
- **Class 038** — streaming transmission / telecommunications services
- **Class 009** — downloadable software / apps

Do not treat these three as the final answer. Counsel should confirm which classes actually cover the store's services (sales platform, streaming, downloads, mobile apps) and whether additional classes are needed.

### 3. Common-law search (unregistered use still creates rights)
- Web search: "DecentralFlix" exact match across search engines; check for active businesses, products, or projects using the name.
- App stores: Apple App Store and Google Play for "DecentralFlix" or confusingly similar names.
- Domain registries: decentralflix.com and major TLD variants — who holds them, since when, and whether they're in use.
- Social handles: major platforms for @decentralflix and variants.
- State business registries in the operating state(s) for conflicting entity names.

### 4. "-Flix" specific risk
The FLIXOLÉ registration shows "Flix"-formative marks can register for streaming — but it does not clear "DecentralFlix". Counsel should specifically assess:
- Likelihood-of-confusion risk against existing "Flix" marks in classes 038/041.
- Whether the "Decentral" prefix is distinctive enough to differentiate, or merely descriptive of the technology (descriptive terms get thin protection and add little distinctiveness).

## Recommendation (from the strategy doc)

Consider a **benefit-led consumer brand** — a name that says what the buyer gets (ownership, keeping films) rather than how the plumbing works — with **DecentralFlix retained as the company or protocol name**. This splits the risk: the consumer mark can be cleared and protected cleanly, while the company name carries the existing project identity.
