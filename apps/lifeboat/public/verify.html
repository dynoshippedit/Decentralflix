Verify a receipt.

Paste a portable receipt below. The Ed25519 signature is checked offline, in your browser — a pure-JavaScript implementation of RFC 8032, no server trust needed. The public key is fetched once from /api/receipts/pubkey; the math after that is all local.

Accepts the wrapped shape {receipt, signature} or the receipt object itself with signature inline.

Entitlement details

    Decentralflix Lifeboat — receipts are signed entitlements: non-transferable, non-cashable, never tokens.
